\# REACLYST



\### An AI Research Platform for Financial Event and Market Impact Analysis



REACLYST is an AI-powered research platform that analyzes financial news, identifies important market events, and studies how financial markets have historically reacted to similar events.



The goal is to connect:



\*\*Financial News → Event Understanding → Historical Similar Events → Market Reaction → AI/ML Analysis\*\*



\## Project Vision



REACLYST is designed as both a software engineering project and an AI/ML research platform.



Rather than simply predicting stock prices, the system focuses on understanding financial events and analyzing their relationship with market behavior.



\## Planned Capabilities



\- Financial news ingestion and processing

\- Company and financial entity extraction

\- Financial event classification

\- Sentiment and contextual analysis

\- Historical market reaction analysis

\- Historical event similarity search

\- Market regime analysis

\- AI/ML experimentation

\- Reproducible experiments and evaluation

\## Research Direction



A central research question for REACLYST is:



> Does event-aware analysis of financial news provide more useful information about subsequent market reactions than conventional sentiment analysis?



The project will investigate this through controlled experiments, baseline comparisons, historical event analysis, and out-of-sample evaluation.
